A visual concept, not a booking service.

Roamly is a fictional travel product. This portfolio exercise has no live listings, inventory, payments, booking functionality, or travel-provider partnerships.

The brief

Travel planning can feel like a pile of disconnected choices. This concept looks at a friendly starting point where a visitor can choose a destination, dates, and group size before exploring trip options.

The direction

Open space, soft coastal colors, and landscape-inspired shapes suggest discovery. A compact search panel keeps the primary planning details visible without competing with the destination imagery.

Website type: travel & booking

  • Destination and trip inspiration
  • A clear search entry point for dates and travelers
  • Room for itinerary, pricing, and booking details in a full product

From concept to a real product

A live booking platform would require real travel inventory, availability, pricing, secure checkout, and integrations. This sample demonstrates only an interface direction; it does not process searches or reservations.
