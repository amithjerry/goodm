A concept, not a client result.

This fictional brand and project were made to explore a possible direction for a skincare storefront. No real client, sales data, or performance claims are represented.

The brief

Imagine a small skincare line with a thoughtful point of view and a product range that is hard to understand at a glance. This exercise asks how a warmer, clearer storefront might help a first-time visitor learn what each product is for.

The direction

A restrained palette, editorial type, and tactile product presentation create a calm first impression. Short, descriptive navigation and clear product groupings are intended to make browsing feel less overwhelming.

What this concept explores

  • Visual identity translated into a digital storefront
  • Clear hierarchy for product discovery and brand storytelling
  • A responsive foundation for future product and editorial pages
