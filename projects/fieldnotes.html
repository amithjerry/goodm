A fictional publication concept.

Fieldnotes is an imaginary editorial project made for this portfolio. The story titles and publication are illustrative; there is no live blog, newsletter, or published editorial archive.

The brief

Independent publications often balance a distinctive point of view with a growing archive of articles. This exercise asks how a homepage can introduce the journal's voice while helping new and returning readers find a story that interests them.

The direction

A warm, print-inspired palette and expressive editorial type make the publication feel personal and focused on its writing. A featured story provides an inviting entry point, while clear topic navigation and consistent reading-time details support browsing.

What this concept explores

  • A publication identity expressed through typography and content hierarchy
  • Featured stories and topic paths for discovering the archive
  • Readable article previews with useful context, such as topic and reading time
  • A flexible layout for long-form articles, author details, and future issues

Designed around the reading experience

A real editorial platform would need an accessible article template, a publishing workflow, search and archive rules, and a consent-based newsletter integration if required. This concept is a visual direction, not a live publication or functioning subscription service.
