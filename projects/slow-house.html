Slow House

An editorial website concept for an imaginary interior design practice. The direction gives a portfolio room to breathe and lets texture, space, and point of view take the lead.

A concept, not a client result.

Slow House is a fictional interior practice created for this portfolio exercise. No real client, completed project, or business outcome is implied.

The brief

For the exercise, the practice is imagined as a small interior studio working with natural materials and personal spaces. Its site needs to introduce its sensibility while leaving the work—not a long pitch—as the main event.

The direction

Soft neutrals, layered architectural shapes, and simple type set a warm tone. An editorial layout provides a framework for a real project portfolio, with room for photography and useful project context. A personal journal adds a quieter voice, sharing notes on materials, spaces, and the rituals of home.

What this concept explores

  • An image-led portfolio structure for a visual discipline
  • A personal blog for reflections on interiors, materials, and everyday living
  • Warm art direction without sacrificing readable contrast
  • A flexible starting point for individual project stories

Built around your actual work

A commissioned website would be shaped around your own photography, process, audience, and business goals. This imagined layout is a starting point for discussion, not a finished client solution.
