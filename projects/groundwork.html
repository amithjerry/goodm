A fictional business concept.

Groundwork is an imaginary consultancy created for this portfolio exercise. No real business, client engagement, customer testimonial, or commercial result is represented.

The brief

Small business owners often know they need strategic help but struggle to tell which service is right for them. This concept explores a more useful first visit: explain the consultancy's value in plain language, make the offer easy to compare, and give visitors a low-pressure next step.

The direction

A grounded palette and confident typography give the brand a capable, approachable feel. A direct headline leads into a short explanation, while service and contact links stay easy to find. The tone aims to feel experienced without sounding corporate or overpromising.

What this concept explores

  • A business homepage organized around audience needs and clear services
  • Trust-building through a transparent process rather than invented testimonials
  • Useful calls to action that help a prospective client know what to do next
  • A responsive content structure that can support service and about pages

Clarity before conversion

A real consultancy website would need discovery with its team and customers, accurate service details, and a considered content strategy. This visual concept has no live enquiry workflow or validated business results.
