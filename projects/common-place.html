A concept, not a client result.

This fictional studio and website were created as an independent portfolio exploration. They do not represent commissioned work or measured business outcomes.

The brief

For this exercise, the studio is imagined as a small team that values collaboration, experimentation, and good questions. The challenge is to communicate personality without making the website hard to navigate.

The direction

Large editorial type and a warm, paper-like palette give the concept its character. A simple navigation structure and generous spacing keep the experience legible while leaving room for the studio's work to lead.

What this concept explores

  • A distinctive first impression without a complicated interface
  • Editorial storytelling for a creative services business
  • A flexible layout that could grow with future studio projects

Your story should lead the design

A real studio's identity, audience, and portfolio would shape a different solution. The concept is a conversation starter—not a template or a claim about a real client.
