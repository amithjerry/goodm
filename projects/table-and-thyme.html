A fictional restaurant concept.

Table & Thyme is an imaginary business created to demonstrate a restaurant website direction. It does not represent a real venue, menu, reservation system, or client engagement.

The brief

Imagine a neighborhood restaurant whose visitors need to quickly answer three questions: what is on the menu, where is the restaurant, and how can I book a table? This concept prioritizes those essentials while still setting an inviting tone.

The direction

A warm palette and editorial typography create the feeling of a printed menu. The information architecture puts menu browsing, visiting details, and reservations within easy reach on desktop or mobile.

Website type: restaurant & hospitality

  • A landing page that introduces the place and its point of view
  • A readable, update-friendly menu and hours section
  • Clear location, contact, and reservation calls to action

Ready to make it yours?

A real restaurant project could connect a reservation service, map, menus, accessibility details, and your own photography. This sample is a visual direction, not a live reservation website.
